Abstract Note:
In formal academic terms: this visualization instantiates a conceptual framework in which obstruction indices are treated as measurable fields encoding impediments to methodological transfer between problem domains. The construction abstracts complex proof‑theoretic resistance into a spatial metaphor—spheres indexed by obstruction values, volumetric heatmaps, and stochastic proof‑path simulations—allowing comparison of relative difficulty and structural relationships. While the model is deliberately reductive and heuristic, it serves as a useful exploratory instrument for hypothesis generation, pedagogical illustration, and qualitative analysis of interproblem connectivity; quantitative claims about actual solvability are not asserted and should be interpreted with circumspection.
